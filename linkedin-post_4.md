📊 I just built my first Meta Ads campaign plan — from objective to KPIs.

As part of the Digital Marketing track, I designed a hypothetical lead-generation campaign for a local gym in Kondapur, Hyderabad, offering a free 3-day trial.

Here's what I planned:
🎯 Objective: Leads (Instant Form) — because the business needs phone numbers to book trial visits, not just clicks
👥 Audience: 3 cold ad sets (broad local, fitness interests, working professionals near HITEC City) + 1 retargeting ad set
🎨 Creatives: 2 feed ads (offer + social proof) and 1 Story/Reel ad, designed for mobile
💰 Budget: ₹15,000 for 28 days — ₹4,200 to test for a week, then scale the winner
📈 KPIs: Cost per Lead (target ≤ ₹150), CTR, CPM, frequency, and cost per new member

Biggest lessons:
1️⃣ The objective should match the business outcome — Traffic and Leads optimise for very different people
2️⃣ Start with a small test budget and let data pick the winner
3️⃣ CPL is the headline number, but CTR and frequency explain *why* it's good or bad
